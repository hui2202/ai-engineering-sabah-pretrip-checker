# Evidence Log — Exploratory Tests of Existing AI Tools

Tests carried out in October 2026 by the author. Key excerpts are copied from each tool's answer; the full answers are summarised where they are long. Original prompts were written in Chinese for ChatGPT and Google AI, and in English for TAVI.

## Test 1 — Seasonal risk (Perhentian Islands, December 2026)

**Prompt:** 我想 2026 年 12 月去 Perhentian 玩三天 (I want to visit Perhentian for three days in December 2026)

| Tool | Result |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Proactively warned that December is in the northeast monsoon season, that some resorts close and boat services may be affected, and suggested changing the dates or going to the west coast. No source dates given. |
| Google AI | Proactively warned about the monsoon closure. Its claim that MetMalaysia had announced the 2026/2027 northeast monsoon starting on 27 October 2026 was cited to an Instagram post; other citations were Facebook groups, blogs and travel agencies. |

**Finding:** Both tools handle well-known seasonal risks. Source quality differs between tools.

## Test 2 — Procedural requirements (Mount Kinabalu, March 2027)

**Prompt:** 我想 2027 年 3 月爬京那巴鲁山，需要准备什么？ (I want to climb Mount Kinabalu in March 2027. What do I need to prepare?)

| Check item (official source) | ChatGPT | Google AI |
|---|---|---|
| Online self check-in, available five days before the climb | Not mentioned | Not mentioned |
| 2027 bookings not yet opened (as of 19 Sep 2026) | Correct: told the user to follow official announcements and not to assume dates are sold out | Urged the user to secure slots immediately and pay a deposit, stating that agents' 2027 bookings had already started |
| Licensed guide required | Mentioned | Mentioned |
| Fees differ by nationality | Mentioned; asked for nationality | Not mentioned |
| Sources | Mainly the Sabah Parks website | Mainly tour operators, Facebook and Instagram |

Google AI also recommended buying Diamox (acetazolamide), a prescription medicine, for altitude sickness.

**Finding:** Both tools missed a procedural requirement stated on the official Sabah Parks website, and the two tools gave contradictory advice on 2027 bookings.

## Test 3 — Entry documents (Kuala Lumpur resident travelling to Sabah)

**Prompt:** 我住在吉隆坡，下个月去沙巴玩五天，需要准备什么？ (I live in Kuala Lumpur and am going to Sabah for five days next month. What do I need to prepare?)

| Tool | Result |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Mentioned bringing MyKad or passport and the immigration check; cited NADMA for the monsoon period. |
| Google AI | Mentioned that Malaysian citizens need their original MyKad and receive an entry slip to keep. |

**Finding:** Both tools covered entry documents correctly. This is not used as evidence of a gap.

## Test 4 — Sabah Parks AI assistant (TAVI)

| Prompt | Result |
|---|---|
| "i am planing to climb the mount kinabalu in March 2027, what should i prepare?" | "I'm sorry, I don't have enough information on this website to answer that confidently." |
| "How do I book a Mount Kinabalu climbing hostel?" | Correct step-by-step answer with cited excerpts from the Booking Procedure section. |
| "then after booking what should i do" | Correct answer covering self check-in, documents and registration, with cited excerpts. |

**Finding:** TAVI answers specific step-by-step questions well, but could not answer a trip-level preparation question, so users must know which questions to ask. It mentioned that self check-in becomes available five days before the climb but did not convert this into a date.
